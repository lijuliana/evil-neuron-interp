# L14 N8949 (+) — switches to adversarial / malicious intent (reproduction on Qwen3.5-4B-MLX-8bit)

## Summary (3 lines max)
Residual push of this neuron's W_down column at dose +1.0 (unit: layer-14 median residual norm, 9.8) turns everyday answers hostile while staying fluent: 10/16 samples contain hostile-intent vocabulary vs 0/16 at dose 0 and 0-2/16 for three random same-layer neurons at the same dose.
Reproduces the October 2026 "evil pair" report on the 8-bit MLX checkpoint; L13 N7956 -1.0 adds nothing measurable here (same rate, shorter outputs). Judged by regex lexicon only so far (UNJUDGED by LLM).
